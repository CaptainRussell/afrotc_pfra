2 km layout on a 300 m track

Two groups running at once from opposite sides. Both run counterclockwise, and each group finishes on its own line.

Group A

Finish: existing finish line

Start: 100 m / 328.1 ft forward from A finish

Group B

Finish: wheel 150 m / 492.1 ft from A finish

Start: 100 m / 328.1 ft forward from B finish

(or 250 m / 820.2 ft from A finish)

Confirm 1 lap 300 m / 984.3 ft

Wheel in lane 1, 30 cm (12 in) out from the inside edge. Each start is wheeled 328.1 ft forward (with the running direction) from its own finish line, which puts it one full curve around, at the near end of the opposite straight. Runners then run 200 m to their first crossing, then 6 full laps. The drawing assumes 50 m straights and 100 m curves; on a track with different straight lengths the marks shift slightly, but the wheel distances stay the same.
