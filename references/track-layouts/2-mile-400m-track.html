2 mile layout on a 400 m track

Two groups running at once from opposite sides. Both run counterclockwise, and each group finishes on its own line.

Group A

Finish: existing finish line

Start: 18.7 m / 61.3 ft back from A finish

Group B

Finish: wheel 200 m / 656.2 ft from A finish

Start: 18.7 m / 61.3 ft back from B finish

(or 181.3 m / 594.9 ft from A finish)

Confirm 1 lap 400 m / 1312.3 ft

Wheel in lane 1, 30 cm (12 in) out from the inside edge. Group B's finish is wheeled counterclockwise from A's, half a lap around. Each start is wheeled 61.3 ft backward (against the running direction) from its own finish line, so both starts sit on a straight. Runners cross their finish line after 18.7 m, then run 8 full laps. The drawing assumes 84 m straights and 116 m curves; on a track with different straight lengths the marks shift slightly, but the wheel distances stay the same.
