Wheel in lane 1, 30 cm (12 in) out from the inside edge. Group B's finish is wheeled counterclockwise from A's, half a lap around. Each start is wheeled 46.0 ft backward (against the running direction) from its own finish line, so both starts sit on a straight. Runners cross their finish line after 14.0 m, then run 8 full laps. The drawing assumes 50 m straights and 100 m curves; on a track with different straight lengths the marks shift slightly, but the wheel distances stay the same.
