2 km layout on a 400 m track

Two groups running at once from opposite sides. Both run counterclockwise, and each group finishes on its own line.

Group A

Start and finish: existing finish line

No wheeling needed

Group B

Start and finish: wheel 200 m / 656.2 ft from A finish

(the painted 200 m start, if your track has one)

Confirm 1 lap 400 m / 1,312.3 ft

2 km is exactly 5 laps of a 400 m track, so each group starts and finishes on the same line and there is nothing to mark for Group A. Group B's line is half a lap around, at the end of the back straight. Wheel it in lane 1, 30 cm (12 in) out from the inside edge, or use the painted 200 m start if the track has one and it is measured to lane 1. Runners finish on their 5th crossing after the start.
