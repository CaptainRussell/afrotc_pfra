      The Rules Behind These Numbers

      DAFMAN 36-2905 and NOTACC CY26-092
      PFRA Proctor Details

      An AED has to be there

      Official assessments and PT are run with an automated external defibrillator present, inside the building or within 100 feet, and it stays on site until everyone has left the area. If there is no AED, the assessment does not run.

      AFROTCI 36-2011 V3, para 10.6.2, citing DAFMAN 36-2905

      How the day runs

      The Fitness Screening Questionnaire is completed before anything else, and may be re-accomplished if something has changed since. On a cadet's FSQ, questions 2 and 3 do not apply: cadets are not sickle cell tested until their first duty station. All three physical components are done inside a 3 hour window on the same day. Up to a 3 minute warm-up is allowed before the run, the HAMR or the walk.

      Body composition may be measured separately from the rest, by no more than 5 duty days. Outside that window the assessment counts as a did-not-finish and has to be done again.

      DAFMAN 36-2905 paras 3.15.9, 3.4.3 and Attachment 2. The 2023 AFROTC Supplement says 3 days, but it predates this assessment and HQ AFROTC has said it will be revised.

      If you are on a medical restriction

      Cadets are deferred from the assessment while on Medical Restriction Status. On release there is a 42 day reconditioning period, and you cannot be made to test during it, though you may choose to, in which case the assessment counts. The requirement is to pass no later than the 60th calendar day after removal from MRS.

      AFROTCI 36-2011 V3, paras 8.7.2 and 8.7.2.1

      Before the assessment can be official

      The 2 mile run course has to be measured and certified with a measuring wheel first, or in coordination with the university. A sports physical is valid for one calendar year from the date of examination.

      NOTACC CY26-092, AY2026-2027 Fitness Guidance

      After an Unsatisfactory

      A cadet has 42 days before being required to test again. You may choose to retest inside that window, but cadre must counsel you first that it counts as an official assessment and that you are giving up the 42 days.

      AFROTC Supplement para 5.5.1.4, from the 2023 supplement, which is awaiting revision. Nothing newer replaces it.
